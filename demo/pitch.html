The Problem

The Accessibility Wall in African Tech Education

Why 3.5M+ Ethiopians with disabilities are excluded from modern tech training:

01

Media Inaccessibility

Course videos lack Ethiopian Sign Language (EthSL) and Amharic captions. Blind learners face screen reader traps on non-semantic video players.

02

Bandwidth & Cost Barriers

Heavy video streaming exhausts mobile 3G data. Commercial speech-to-text APIs cost $3.60/hr with zero EthSL support and fail completely offline.

03

Inaccessible Career Portals

Job boards fail at 200% screen magnifier zoom, lack disability accommodation disclosure, and exclude local Ethiopian calendar timelines.

Co-Design & Architecture

Validated with Real Co-Designers in Addis Ababa

Iterative improvements from testing with blind, deaf, and low-vision learners:

👥 Co-Designer User Feedback

Android TalkBack Timing: TalkBack dropped subtitle updates. Fixed by adding a 60ms delay buffer and polite ARIA live regions so screen reader users hear captions synchronously.

Motor Impairment Alternative: Dragging the EthSL sign window was difficult for users with tremors. Added discrete +/- buttons meeting WCAG 2.5.7.

Astronomical Ethiopian Calendar: Replaced inaccurate leap-year math with Julian Day astronomical epoch and Geez numeral rendering (e.g., ፳፻፲፱ ዓ.ም.).

⚙️ Offline-First Architecture

Zero External CDN Dependency: Standalone bundle is only 144 KB. Embeds into any static HTML page without npm installs or build steps.

3G Data-Saver & Offline Pack: Automatically pauses video stream, generates an offline ZIP pack under 1MB with English Grade 1 Braille and accessible PDF.

Resilient Cryptographic Fallback: If backend goes offline, client-side Ed25519 engine validates certificates and detects tampered student names.
