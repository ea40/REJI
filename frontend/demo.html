Use cases

Privacy-first adaptive presets that dynamically reconfigure media delivery to match learner needs and Ethiopian network constraints without blocking popups.

Deaf & Hard of Hearing

Synchronizes dual English and Amharic Ethiopic captions at 150% scale, attaches the Ethiopian Sign Language (EthSL) picture-in-picture stream, and outputs visual sound indicators for non-speech events (e.g. [ሙዚቃ], [ጭብጨባ]).

Blind & Screen Reader

Collapses decorative controls into clean semantic landmarks, activates spoken Audio Description with -14dB primary track ducking via Web Audio, and produces standard 40-cell Grade 1 ASCII Braille (.brf) transcripts locally.

One-Hand & Motor-Friendly

Enforces WCAG 2.5.5 touch target sizing (≥48×48px with 12px clearance), provides one-click corner-snap docking presets, and routes single-key shortcuts (Space, J, K, L, M, C, S, A) with discrete ±15s step jump buttons.

Low-Bandwidth 3G Mode (Ethio Telecom / Safaricom)

Passively interrogates network connection APIs to halt high-bitrate video streaming on Ethio Telecom / Safaricom 2G/3G tiers, serving compressed speech audio, interactive synchronized text, and zero-data offline ZIP bundles.
